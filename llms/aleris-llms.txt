# Aleris

> Aleris was a global producer of rolled aluminum products for the aerospace, automotive,
> building and construction, and other industrial end-use markets. It no longer exists as an
> independent company: Novelis Inc. (a subsidiary of Hindalco Industries) completed its
> acquisition of Aleris Corporation on 2020-04-14 for approximately $2.8 billion. Aleris
> published no developer program, no API documentation, and no machine-readable API contract,
> and its corporate domain no longer serves a website.

## Status

- Defunct as an independent brand. Acquired by Novelis Inc., completed 2020-04-14.
- aleris.com still resolves in DNS but does not answer HTTP on port 80 or 443 (probed 2026-09-04).
- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, gRPC/Protobuf, WSDL, MCP server or A2A agent card was found on
  any host associated with this record.
- No /.well-known/ discovery document, no /apis.json, and no llms.txt on any Aleris host.
- Manufacturing-sector B2B integration at this company ran through EDI with named trading
  partners, which is a private bilateral surface and not a published API.

## Successor

- [Novelis Inc.](https://novelis.com) — the acquirer and the surviving corporate entity. Any API,
  llms.txt or discovery document served from novelis.com belongs to Novelis, not to Aleris, and is
  deliberately not claimed on this record.
